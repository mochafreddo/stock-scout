# QA workflow

Terms used to distinguish the outcome of a QA investigation from the behavior of the product under review.

## Language

**Review completion**:
Whether the planned investigation has been accounted for. A complete review can report unresolved product failures or blocked checks; a partial review leaves planned investigation unfinished.

**Product verdict**:
Whether the required contracts for the selected scope and inputs pass. The verdict is passing when all pass, failing when any required contract fails, and unverified when none fail but required evidence is missing.
